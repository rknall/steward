Builds, upgrades, pauses and buffs the mines of your deposits, and buffs the masons, on your home island.

What you can do

You want a mine built on every deposit that has none.

Steps
  1. Open the Steward dashboard (Open Dashboard… in the ~Steward~ menu) and select the Mining tab.
  2. In the Mining card, turn on Build, upgrade and buff mines.
  3. In the Mines per deposit table, check that Build is on for the deposits you want.
  4. Click Save, and stay on your home island.
Result
Every deposit of those types with no building on its grid gets a mine, as long as the build queue has a free slot, you have a building licence left and you can afford the mine. A grid that already holds any building, even a depleted one, is left alone. The module is off until you turn it on.

You want your mines raised to a level you choose.

Steps
  1. In the Mines per deposit table, turn on Upgrade for the deposit.
  2. Set Up to level to the level you want (any whole number from 1).
  3. Click Save.
Result
Mines below that level are upgraded. The game decides how high a mine can go, so a level above its real maximum just stops there.

You want a mine paused before its deposit runs dry.

Steps
  1. Set Pause threshold in the Mining card: the amount a deposit may fall to.
  2. In the Mines per deposit table, turn on Pause for the deposit.
  3. Click Save.
Result
A producing mine whose deposit has less than the threshold left is paused. Steward only pauses: it never resumes a mine, and with Pause off it leaves your own pauses alone.

You want a production buff kept on your mines and masons.

Steps
  1. In the Buff column of the Mines per deposit table, pick a buff from the list for the deposit. Masons for stone, marble and granite are in the MASONS block below it.
  2. Click Save.
  3. Check the line under the list: it shows the stock and how many buildings are buffed.
Result
The buff is applied to every mine or mason of that type that is producing, has no buff and is not being upgraded, a few at a time, and each apply is checked on the building. The list offers only the buffs the game offers for that building and shows how much each gives; None switches buffing off for the type. Steward does not build masons, it only buffs them.

Settings

The module switch, the delay and the threshold are on the Mining card; the per-deposit settings are the columns of the Mines per deposit table. Everything takes effect after Save.

enabled

Default: Off

The Build, upgrade and buff mines switch. When off, the module does nothing.

Change it when you want steward to manage your mines, or to stop.

actionDelay

Default: 1500 ms

The Delay between actions: the pause between two actions queued in one pass.

Change it when you want a gentler or a faster pace.

pauseThreshold

Default: 50 units

The Pause threshold, shared by all deposits: a mine with Pause on is paused once its deposit has less than this left.

Change it when you want mines stopped earlier or later.

deposits.enabled

Default: On for all nine deposit types

Whether the module manages that deposit type at all. It is stored per deposit and has no control on the tab.

Change it when you want a type left completely alone.

deposits.build

Default: On for the six mine types (stone, marble and granite have no mine)

The Build column: place a mine on deposits of the type that have none.

Change it when you want to place mines yourself.

deposits.upgrade

Default: Off

The Upgrade column: raise mines of the type up to the level below.

Change it when you want steward to level your mines.

deposits.targetLevel

Default: 3

The Up to level column: the level Upgrade aims for. Any whole number from 1; the game limits the real top level.

Change it when you want higher or lower mines.

deposits.pause

Default: Off

The Pause column: pause producing mines of the type when their deposit falls under the Pause threshold. It only pauses; it never resumes.

Change it when you want to stretch the life of a deposit.

deposits.buff

Default: None (off) for all nine types

The Buff column: the production buff applied to the type's mines, or to its masons for stone, marble and granite. A stored buff the game no longer offers stays in the list, marked not offered, and is never applied.

Change it when you want buildings of that type buffed.

deposits.refill

Default: off (empty)

Reserved for a refill step that is not built yet. It does nothing and has no control on the tab.

Change it when never, for now.

buffVersion

Default: 1

An internal marker. Buff choices stored before buffing existed are cleared once at start, and this marker records that it was done. A fresh install already carries it.

Change it when never; leave it as it is.

Notes

  • Each pass works deposit by deposit in the order build, upgrade, pause, buff.
  • The Mining card shows the build queue (slots used of total) and your licences, plus Buffed now and the Buff breaker.
  • If buffs are applied but not seen on their building several times in a row, the breaker opens and the tab shows a "Buffing paused" notice. Only buffing stops; building, upgrading and pausing keep running. It tries again with one probe every few minutes, and reloading scripts resets it.
  • A building whose buff apply was not seen is held for a while, because the buff may have landed unseen.
  • The Geologists module finds the deposits; this module builds on them on a later pass.
