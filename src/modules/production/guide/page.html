Keeps your timed production queues busy: when a building's queue is empty it orders the item you chose, and it gives the building a buff if you want one.

What you can do

You want a building to start the same item again whenever its queue runs empty.

Steps
  1. Open the production manager: in the ~Steward~ menu choose Production, then Open production manager…. The button of the same name on the Production tab of the dashboard does the same.
  2. In the window Steward production, stay on the Production Manager tab and pick the chip All buildings.
  3. On the row of the building, choose an item in Item · cost and set Items, the number of runs in one order.
  4. Turn on Enable for that row and click Save.
  5. Turn on Auto-production in the Production submenu, or on the Production tab, and save there.
Result
The row now appears under the chip Automatic queues. Whenever that building's queue holds nothing, steward orders the item with the Items value, and checks that the order shows up in the queue. A buff that is made so fast that it reaches your star menu before the queue shows it also counts as seen. The cost shown in the row is for one order. The module is off until you turn on Auto-production, and it only works on your home island.

You want a production buff on the buildings steward just put to work.

Steps
  1. In the production manager, pick a buff in the Buff column of the row. Only buffs the game offers for that building and that you have in stock are listed.
  2. Click Save.
  3. Keep Building buffs on in the Production submenu.
Result
Steward buffs a building only when it has no production buff running, whichever buff that is, and never replaces or tops up a running one. Both Building buffs and Automatic queues must be on. A row with a buff but no item is never buffed. Mines and masons stay with the Mining module. What happens depends on the queue:
  • Queue empty and no buff running: the order is placed with the buff.
  • Queue empty and a buff running (the same or another): the order is placed without a buff.
  • The row's item is being produced (it is first in the queue and not finished) and no buff runs, for example because the buff ran out: the buff is applied to the running queue. At most Buffs per pass buffs go out per pass, shared with the order buffs, each using one buff from your stock.
  • Other items are being produced and no buff runs: no buff. If your item is queued behind them, the buff comes once your item is first in the queue.
  • If steward cannot read the buff, the queue or the item being produced, it waits and does not buff.
This applies to every building with a production queue, for example the Rarity Provision House and the Bookbinder, and the buff that comes with an order goes out on all of them. When steward holds back a buff it writes one line to the log, such as cannot take <buff> now: queue not producing or queue state unreadable, once per building until the building can take a buff again.

You want to switch parts of production on or off quickly.

Steps
  1. Open the ~Steward~ menu and its Production submenu.
  2. Click Auto-production, Automatic queues or Building buffs.
Result
A tick means on, a cross means off. A click takes effect at once, with no Save. The line under Auto-production shows the number of queues and entries, when the next one is done and whether the breaker is ok or open. Turning the module off stops only its own work; adventure runs keep ordering their own items.

You want to see what every production queue holds right now.

Steps
  1. In the window Steward production, open the Queues tab.
  2. Use the chips Producing and All to narrow the list, and Refresh to read again.
Result
Each queue is a card of entries with Item, Amount, Produced, Remaining, Done at and Source, and says which automatic queue it has. The tab is read when it opens and on Refresh, and never redraws on its own.

You want finished books collected for you.

Steps
  1. In the production manager, enable the Bookbinder row.
  2. On the Settings tab of the window, check that Bookbinder auto-complete is on.
Result
Finished books of the Bookbinder queue are collected, one per pass, while that row is enabled. The switch on the Settings tab applies at once.

Settings

The switches and numbers of the module are on the Production tab of the dashboard and in the Production submenu. The per-building rows are in the production manager window.

enabled

Default: Off

The Auto-production switch. When off, the module orders and buffs nothing.

Change it when you want steward to keep queues filled, or to stop.

actionDelay

Default: 1500 ms

The Delay between actions: the pause between two actions queued in one pass.

Change it when you want a gentler or a faster pace.

ordersPerPass

Default: 2

The Orders per pass: at most this many orders per pass, from 1 to 5. Each verified send holds the module for up to 15 seconds.

Change it when many queues are empty and you want them filled in fewer passes.

buffsPerPass

Default: 2

The Buffs per pass: at most this many buffs per pass, from 1 to 5.

Change it when you want buffs sent faster or slower.

standing

Default: On

The Automatic queues switch. Gates all automatic orders; a row that is off still never acts.

Change it when you want to pause automatic orders but keep your rows.

buffs

Default: On

The Building buffs switch. Gates the buff sent with the orders and the buff given back to a building that is producing the row's item. Mines and masons stay with the Mining module.

Change it when you want orders without buffs.

bookComplete

Default: On

The Bookbinder auto-complete switch on the Settings tab of the window: collect the finished Bookbinder queue. It only acts while the Bookbinder row is enabled.

Change it when you want to collect books yourself.

buildings

Default: no rows

One row per building name, edited in the production manager. A building with no row has nothing configured. The queue runs at your highest-level building of that name, which is the only one ordered into or buffed.

Change it when you add or change what a building produces.

buildings.item

Default: none (off)

The item in Item · cost that is ordered into an idle queue. An item the game would not let you order now is not ordered.

Change it when you want the building to make something else.

buildings.items

Default: 1

The Items column: how many runs go into one order. It is cut down to what the queue allows; a queue that takes one run shows a fixed 1, and books go one at a time.

Change it when you want bigger or smaller orders.

buildings.buff

Default: none (off)

The Buff column: the buff sent with an automatic order, or given again to a building that is producing the row's item, while the building has no production buff running. Needs Automatic queues on.

Change it when you want that building buffed.

buildings.enabled

Default: Off for a new row

The Enable switch of the row. It gates the order, the buff and, for the Bookbinder row, the collection.

Change it when you want a row to start or stop without losing its settings.

settingsVersion

Default: 2

An internal marker for the layout of the stored settings, so older stored settings are migrated once at start.

Change it when never; leave it as it is.

Notes

  • Only buildings with a production queue are covered. Farms and sawmills without a queue, and mines and masons, are not.
  • The window refuses to open over unsaved dashboard or Settings changes, and the dashboard refuses to open over unsaved changes in the window. A new zone closes the window, so an unsaved draft is dropped.
  • A buff that has run out of stock reads None in the list. A Save writes what the window shows, so it clears such a buff on every row, and the change note names it.
  • An order that does not show up in its queue holds that building for 30 minutes. Five misses in a row open the breaker and pause all automatic queues; the dashboard then shows a "Production paused" banner with a Reset link, and the module tries again by itself later. Buffs have a breaker of their own.
  • An adventure run keeps the resources it needs, so an automatic queue waits when a run holds them.
  • The Overview tab has a Production tile with a Manager link.
