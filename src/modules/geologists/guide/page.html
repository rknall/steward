Sends your idle geologists to search for deposits, so the map keeps the number of deposits you want.

What you can do

You want geologists sent out whenever there are fewer deposits on your island than you want.

Steps
  1. Open the Steward dashboard (Open Dashboard… in the ~Steward~ menu) and select the Geologists tab.
  2. In the Geologists card, turn on Send geologists.
  3. In the DEPOSITS TO SEARCH table, switch Search on for each deposit you want, and set Max, the number to keep on the map.
  4. Click Save, and stay on your home island.
Result
For each deposit you search, steward counts what is on the map plus what geologists are already searching for. If that is below Max, it sends the best idle geologist for the missing ones, one send after another with the Delay between sends pause. A number in the On map column is marked when it is below Max. The module is off until you turn it on.

You want a kind of geologist kept for certain deposits only.

Steps
  1. In the DEPOSITS TO SEARCH table, click the word default after the Search switch of a deposit.
  2. In the row that opens, tick the kinds of geologist that may search this deposit. Each chip shows how many geologists you own of that kind.
  3. Click Save. To undo, open the row again and click Back to default.
Result
The word reads override for that deposit. Only the ticked kinds search it, and a kind ticked for any deposit is kept for the deposits that list it and stays idle otherwise. A kind covers every geologist of that kind, now and later.

You want to see which geologist is best for which deposit, before or while the module runs.

Steps
  1. On the Geologists tab, read the Live state table: Geologist, Status, Best deposit and Searching.
  2. Use the chips All, Busy, Idle and With a trait to narrow it.
  3. Click Show ranking in log for a per-deposit ranking in the log.
Result
Best deposit shows the deposit a trait favours, with its amount and time factors. Show ranking in log runs at once, with no Save, and ranks only the geologists the module could actually send to each deposit.

You want depleted deposits cleared so the grid frees up for new searches.

Steps
  1. On the Geologists tab, turn on Remove depleted deposits.
  2. Click Save.
Result
Depleted deposit buildings of the deposit types you manage are removed on your home island, one at a time. A deposit that still holds ore is never touched.

Settings

Switches and numbers are changed on the Geologists tab and take effect after Save.

enabled

Default: Off

The Send geologists switch. When off, no geologist is sent and nothing is removed.

Change it when you want steward to take over searching, or to take it back yourself.

dispatchDelay

Default: 1500 ms

The Delay between sends: the pause between two geologists sent in one pass.

Change it when you want a gentler or a faster pace.

removeDepleted

Default: Off

The Remove depleted deposits switch. When on, steward removes depleted deposit buildings of the managed types on the home island, which frees their grid for new searches.

Change it when depleted deposits are blocking space you want searched again.

deposits.enabled

Default: On for every deposit type

The Search switch of a deposit row. A deposit with Search off is never searched for.

Change it when you do not want a deposit type on the map.

deposits.max

Default: Stone 8, bronze ore 6, marble 10, iron ore 19, gold ore 9, coal 6, granite 6, titanium ore 4, salpeter 4

The Max column: how many deposits of the type steward keeps on the map. Searches are sent while the count on the map plus the count being searched for is below it. The defaults are the values used by other TSO helper scripts against the live game.

Change it when you want more or fewer of a deposit for your zone or play style.

deposits.kinds

Default: none, so any geologist whose kind is not reserved may search

The kinds of geologist that may search a deposit, set with the default / override word of the row. Clearing the list brings the deposit back to the default.

Change it when a special kind of geologist should be saved for particular deposits.

overrides

Default: none

Pins a named geologist to a list of deposits: it is only considered for those. A geologist must pass both this and the kind rule above. It is not shown on the tab; it is edited in the settings file by hand.

Change it when one named geologist should only ever search certain deposits.

Notes

  • The best geologist for a deposit is chosen by search amount first and speed second.
  • If the list of geologists could not be read in full, steward sends nothing that pass; the tab then says the geologists could not be read.
  • The Overview tab has a Geologists tile with the busy count and how many deposit types are below their max.
